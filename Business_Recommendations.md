# Business Recommendations

## Cluster 0: High Value Customers

- Provide premium credit card offers.
- Introduce loyalty reward programs.
- Offer exclusive shopping benefits.

## Cluster 1: Low Spending Customers

- Provide cashback offers.
- Introduce shopping discounts.
- Encourage regular purchases.

## Cluster 2: Cash Advance Users

- Promote responsible credit usage.
- Provide financial awareness campaigns.
- Encourage regular purchase activities.

## Cluster 3: Low Credit Limit Customers

- Provide credit management guidance.
- Offer suitable financial products.
- Encourage responsible spending habits.

## Conclusion

Customer segmentation helps the marketing department understand different customer groups and develop targeted marketing strategies.
